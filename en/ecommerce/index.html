Shopping journey

The architecture considers catalog, search, product pages, cart and purchase stages. The design changes depending on whether the sale is direct, by quote or B2B with specific customer and price rules.

Integrated operation

Orders can feed ERP, update stock and trigger financial and commercial flows, depending on the available interfaces. Payment gateways and third-party services require analysis of fees, security, reconciliation, and responsibilities for each system.

Data-driven evolution

Navigation and conversion measurement helps find real friction. Mobile performance, SEO of relevant pages and catalog maintenance matter as much as the store's first publication.

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to integrate e-commerce with ERP?

Yes, if there are technical means of exchanging data. We map products, stock, orders and update rules before implementing.

Do I need my own platform?

It depends on the sales rules and integrations. An existing platform may be sufficient; own development makes sense for specific needs.

Can the catalog have B2B rules?

Yes, such as visibility per customer, specific prices or requests for quotation, when provided for in the scope.
