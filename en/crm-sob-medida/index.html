Visibility from first contact to closing

Organize leads, customers, opportunities and history in one flow. The pipeline can reflect real commercial stages, with responsible parties, tasks, follow-ups, proposals and goals. Profiles and permissions help each salesperson and manager see what they need, without losing control over the data.

Useful indicators and automations

Dashboards can show conversion by stage, pending activities, results by team and evolution of proposals. Automations reduce forgetfulness: notices, lead distribution and status updates can be designed according to business rules, with clear criteria to avoid noise.

Integration and deployment

WhatsApp, email, ERP and other APIs can be part of the architecture, depending on the availability of each platform. Before implementation, we define the origin of the data, cleaning of records, user access and necessary training. Thus, CRM enters the business process with reliable information.

Frequently asked questions

What is a bespoke CRM?

It is a relationship and sales system designed for the company's specific funnel, rules and indicators.

How much does it cost to develop a CRM?

Depends on modules, integrations, data migration and number of profiles. The proposal is made after mapping the commercial process.

Is it possible to integrate with WhatsApp and ERP?

In many scenarios, yes. Feasibility depends on the APIs, permissions and rules of the platforms used.

Can I migrate data from another CRM?

Migration can be planned after analyzing export, quality and structure of existing data.
