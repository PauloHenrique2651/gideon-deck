Concrete use cases

Request classification, document search, service support and assisted data analysis are possible starting points. Each case needs an objective, source of information and evaluation criteria. Not every process benefits from an autonomous agent.

Integration with real work

An AI solution can query authorized databases, record actions in CRM or ERP and forward exceptions to people. Permissions, privacy, costs per use, and response limits are handled as part of the product.

Test before zooming

We propose to validate the quality of answers with real examples, measure errors and adjust instructions and data. Deployment can begin assisted, with human review, before automating more sensitive actions.

Frequently asked questions

What can an AI agent do in the company?

Can support defined tasks, such as querying information, classifying inputs or triggering flows, within established permissions and limits.

Can AI access my ERP or CRM?

It can, if there is authorized integration and clear rules for reading, writing and auditing data.

How to assess whether it is worth it?

It is necessary to compare quality, time saved, operating cost and process risk before expanding the solution.
