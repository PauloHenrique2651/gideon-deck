Message and conversion

We define the audience, traffic source and objective before the layout. The page can prioritize budget, contact via WhatsApp or registration, depending on the commercial process. Text, visual hierarchy and CTA must make it clear what the person will receive when moving forward.

Lean implementation

Responsiveness, fast loading, accessibility and integration with relevant analytics or pixels are considered from the beginning. Forms need a reliable destination and sending confirmation; lead events should only fire when the send actually works.

SEO when it makes sense

Temporary campaign pages do not always need indexing. When the offer responds to a recurring search, we plan our own content, stable URL and internal links, avoiding competing with existing service pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is a landing page the same as a website?

No. The landing page usually has an offer and a main action; a website presents the company and its different solutions.

Can you receive paid and organic traffic?

Yes, but the content and indexing strategy must consider the intention of each source.

How to measure results?

Click events, confirmed sends, and campaign data help you analyze conversion without counting failed actions.
