Modules aligned to the routine

The project can bring together registrations, orders, purchases, stock movements, accounts payable and receivable, approvals and reports. Users and permissions follow team responsibilities. The rules are defined with whoever executes the process, so that the system truly represents the operation.

Data to decide

Dashboards help monitor sales, margin, financial flow, inventory turnover and points of attention. Integrations with e-commerce, CRM, payment methods and external APIs avoid duplicate records. The data source and synchronization frequency are decided in the technical drawing.

Responsible implementation

An ERP touches critical routines. Therefore, we map existing systems, tax dependencies, quality of registrations and migration plan. Fiscal features, such as issuing documents, require specific analysis of applicable rules and providers; are not assumed in every project.

Frequently asked questions

What is a tailored ERP?

It is a management system developed to bring together company-specific processes and rules, rather than imposing a standard flow.

Can ERP integrate inventory, finance and sales?

Yes. These modules can share data, as long as the scope and operational rules are defined in the project.

Can the ERP issue invoices?

The possibility depends on tax requirements, integrations with providers and applicable legislation. This needs scope analysis.

Is it possible to migrate from another ERP?

Yes, after evaluating data access, format and quality and planning the transition with the team.
