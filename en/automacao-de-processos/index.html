Map before automating

We identify triggers, culprits, exceptions and success criteria. A flow can update the CRM, record an order in the ERP, send a notification, or request approval. The objective is to remove repetitive work without hiding decisions that need to continue with people.

Tools and integrations

APIs, webhooks and platforms like n8n can connect email, WhatsApp, internal systems and external services. The choice depends on volume, security, support and maintainability. AI agents can support classifications or responses as long as there is adequate data, thresholds, and review.

Observable operation

Reliable automation records failures, allows reprocessing and makes it clear who takes action when an integration stops. We document the flow and validate common scenarios and exceptions before putting it into the team's routine.

Frequently asked questions

Which processes can be automated?

Registrations, notifications, approvals, status updates and data synchronization are examples. Priority depends on the volume and cost of rework.

Is it possible to integrate WhatsApp?

Yes, when use respects the technical and political options of the chosen platform.

Does automation work with existing systems?

Many times, yes. We evaluate APIs, webhooks, exports and access restrictions before defining the flow.
